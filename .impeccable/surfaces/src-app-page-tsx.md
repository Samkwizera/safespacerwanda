# Surface brief: site-wide redesign (home leads)

Scope: every route shares one redesigned system; the homepage is the lead surface. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, about, programs, community, get-involved); Read (resources, legal).

Audience and job: young people deciding to join, and organisations/volunteers deciding to help, weighted equally. Two doors on every key page.
Proof/content: existing copy only. No photos, founder bio or events yet; slots stay visible and honest.
Constraints: taglines verbatim; never themed on sport; community-not-clinic disclaimer stays; forms and validation unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, friendly youth-wellbeing home at Headspace's craft level, played straight. It refuses the pastel-blob template: blurred orbs, eyebrow pills, floating badges, marquees and identical scroll reveals.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with large soft colour fields (sky, sun, blush, mint) and deep indigo ink. One bright friendly blue carries actions; sunshine yellow carries the signature shape. Figtree throughout, heavy for headings. Overlapping circle and capsule shapes (two forms, one shared space) stand in for illustration. Pill buttons, 16px cards, no eyebrows.

STORY: The visitor learns this is a youth community for wellbeing in Rwanda, not a clinic, and that they can show up as they are. A young person leaves their email or joins; an organisation finds "Partner with us" without hunting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: the headline at display scale, one paragraph, a working email sign-up (input, button, consent) and a quiet row of the three doors. Right five columns: the breathing-space mark at hero scale, a sun circle and a blue capsule overlapping, their overlap filled ink. A hero photo replaces the capsule fill once one exists.

FORM: Category standard (canon, the user's choice over the dealt directions), position: standing exit. seed:22e7f220b17126ae. Signature interaction: the hero shapes breathe slowly (6s in, 6s out), off for reduced motion. Motion grammar: one hero entrance, press feedback on controls, colour-only hovers; no per-section reveals.
